Correo Limpio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Correo Limpio is a personal tool. It accesses only the mailboxes of its owner, with the owner's permission, to move spam to the trash and remove mail from senders the owner has blocked.

  • No email content is stored, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • Data from Google APIs is used only to perform these cleanup actions, and its use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings.
